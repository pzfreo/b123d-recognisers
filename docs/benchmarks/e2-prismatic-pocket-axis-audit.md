# E2 Prismatic Pocket frame-axis audit

Issue [#321](https://github.com/pzfreo/b123d-recognisers/issues/321) asked whether six raw-to-framed
PrismaticPocket losses exposed a signed principal-axis defect in ring discovery or reconciliation.
They do not. Authored triangular, hexagonal and rectangular rings are covariant over signed local
X/Y/Z. Every affected corpus ring is oblique inside the independently inferred part frame.

- Selection: first 500 STEP model IDs, lexical ascending; selected-ID SHA-256
  `323c956889bf6018f37d8411367c6b30b95ffac8011b13a69f06e189568401df`.
- Historical comparison:
  [`frame-corpus-500-production.json`](frame-corpus-500-production.json), artifact SHA-256
  `ba75a0f5272c0a0dea536abf92874e579cba47977ad4b809c8c866090a21d6ef`.
- Defining-wall audit:
  [`e2-prismatic-pocket-frame-losses.json`](e2-prismatic-pocket-frame-losses.json), generated by
  `tools/audit_prismatic_pocket_frame_losses.py`; artifact SHA-256

The focused tool matches occurrences by their complete defining-wall face sets. For every wall it
validates surface type, edge count, area, transformed centre and transformed normal across the
topology-preserving placement. Four rings have no local axis to which every wall is parallel within
the production `AXIS_ZERO_COS` boundary. The two 0.4° rings remain inside that normal boundary but
their wall bounds no longer share a principal-axis span within `SPAN_EPS`. Direct and aggregate
PrismaticPocket counts agree in both raw and framed shapes, so none of the six losses is caused by
Pocket precedence, another reconciler or the corpus matcher.

## Corpus-independent contract proof

The authored suite exercises triangular and hexagonal recesses along signed X/Y/Z under
translation. Each returns the same side count and 8 mm depth through direct and aggregate routes,
with the expected local axis and opening sign. The same signed matrix proves that a rectangular
ring is discovered directly in every orientation and then consistently reconciled to the more
specific rectangular Pocket in the aggregate. Triangular, hexagonal and rectangular principal-Y
STEP round trips retain those outcomes. Existing tests retain the through-passage distinction,
both cap orientations, multiple occurrence identity, obround exclusion, exact wall provenance and
rectangular precedence. The focused module has 30 passing tests.

## Decision

Close #321 without production code. The principal-axis ring contract is now explicit and tested;
the six historical losses are recognizer rejections of internally oblique rings, which E2 and ADR
0011 exclude. Relaxing wall-axis or equal-span boundaries would broaden the supported geometry and
change shared ring semantics for Passage as well as PrismaticPocket; these models supply no
corpus-independent justification for that change.

No predicate, tolerance, record, provenance or reconciliation code changes, so accepted output and
runtime are unchanged. MFInstSeg is absent and was not inspected; this child is not a transfer
milestone. ADR 0002 determinism and traversal-independent values are strengthened; ADRs 0003 and
0007 retain exact wall evidence and existing Pocket precedence; ADR 0008 gains no tolerance; ADR
0009 gains no hidden filter; and ADR 0011's whole-part framing versus internally oblique feature
boundary is preserved.
