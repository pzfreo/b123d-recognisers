# E2 residual Fillet and Plate frame-axis audit

Issue [#323](https://github.com/pzfreo/b123d-recognisers/issues/323) traced the last three Fillet
and one Plate legacy-to-framed transitions in the first 500 MFCAD++ development models. The
Fillet transitions are one covariance defect with a narrow fix. The Plate transition is a
different, genuine covariance defect in its bbox-based area denominator and is isolated in
[#329](https://github.com/pzfreo/b123d-recognisers/issues/329); this increment does not change
Plate semantics.

## Fillet diagnosis and contract

The cylindrical Fillet route rebuilds the virtual sharp corner from the nearest principal plane
on each transverse axis. When two distinct planes are exactly equidistant, the former deterministic
lower-coordinate tie-break chooses the opposite physical plane after a sign inversion. That can
flip the material probe even though the face and topology are unchanged.

The audit validates target-face identity across normalization by surface type, edge count, area,
transformed centre and transformed normal. All three inferred frames are signed principal-axis
permutations. The historical transitions are:

| Model | Face | MFCAD++ class | Historical transition | Equidistant axis |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| `1129.step` | 11 | Circular end pocket (16) | raw Fillet absent when framed | raw X / local Y |
| `1149.step` | 3 | Circular blind step (21) | raw Fillet absent when framed | both transverse axes |
| `11257.step` | 16 | Circular end pocket (16) | Fillet introduced when framed | raw/local X |

The corpus labels identify these as false positives but do not define the fix. The independent
geometric contract is: a cylindrical edge blend may use the neighbour-plane material probe only
when each transverse physical supporting plane is unique within the existing ADR-0008 local length
tolerance. Split coplanar faces count as one plane; two distinct equidistant planes make this
Fillet route ambiguous and fail it closed. No radius, angular, material or public-record threshold
is relaxed.

The shared query retains its exact historical `(distance, coordinate)` choice for Chamfer and
AngledStep. Applying the ambiguity refusal to every consumer was measured and rejected: it removed
five correctly labelled Angled Steps. Fillet opts into the refusal because it has no additional
terminal or section evidence that can identify one of the tied physical corners.

Tests cover distinct ties, coplanar equivalence, the scale-aware boundary on both sides, closer
planes under both signs, rotated internal pocket rounds, rotated and mirrored through slots, and
the existing positive/negative/order suites. Direct and aggregate Fillet discovery share the same
core, so the gate cannot diverge between public and attributed routes.

## MFCAD++ effect

The exact v2 score-vector difference is deliberately small:

| Metric | Parent | Fillet fix | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Accepted Fillets | 56 | 48 | -8 |
| Mapped class records | unchanged for every class | unchanged | 0 |
| Unmapped accepted records | 783 | 775 | -8 |
| Taxonomy-mismatch defining faces | 1,122 | 1,114 | -8 |
| Empty models | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Invalid models | 0 | 0 | 0 |

All eight removals occur in seven models: `10314`, `10627`, `10683`, `1129`, `1149` (two),
`11905`, and `12029`. Seven defining faces are labelled Circular Blind Step and one Circular End
Pocket. No removed face is labelled Round (class 23), so Round defining-face recall stays `0/1`;
its precision denominator falls from 56 to 48 because the scorer reports every Fillet defining
face against that one-vs-class denominator. Every other family count, mapped-class count,
reconciliation drop and class recall is unchanged.

The same-host isolated parent and changed runs took 223.992 and 228.097 seconds respectively,
a 1.83% total increase. Median moved from 0.408 to 0.432 seconds while p95 fell from 0.825 to
0.790 seconds. This shared-host measurement stays below the 10% paired review threshold and is
not a latency guarantee.

The paired frame census independently removes both legacy-to-framed Fillet absences and the one
Fillet introduction. Framed rigid motion retains zero absences and reclassifications over all 500
models. The one Plate absence and every non-Fillet mismatch remain unchanged; the eight rejected
raw Fillets reduce the physical baseline from 2,901 to 2,893 records. Census wall times are
recorded for reproducibility but were not paired in isolation and are not used as performance
evidence.

## Plate disposition

`10649.step` has one raw Plate with defining faces 4, 9 and 20. Their MFCAD++ labels are Angled
Step, Stock and unsupported Slanted Through Step, so the label set does not establish a true Plate.
The geometric defect nevertheless exists independently of those labels. The inferred frame maps
the Plate normal exactly from raw Z to local X, all defining faces retain principal normals, and
their two group areas remain 565.865609 and 261.266960 square model units. An in-plane 17.439433°
roll expands the axis-aligned bbox cross area from 615.112347 to 970.260549, so the fixed 0.4 gate
moves from 246.044939 to 388.104219. The same physical groups pass raw and fail framed.

Changing the cross-section authority could alter Plate eligibility across the corpus and its
negative space. It therefore does not ride with the Fillet correction. Issue #329 requires a
rotation-invariant physical denominator, sharp and adversarial controls, a full before/after
census, and a second focused review before Plate behavior changes.

## ADR disposition

ADR 0002 determinism is preserved: the legacy query remains traversal-independent and the Fillet
ambiguity path is coordinate-sign independent. ADR 0007 is preserved because one Fillet discovery
core owns both direct records and aggregate evidence. ADR 0008 supplies the existing scale-aware
length comparison and gains boundary tests rather than a corpus-fitted constant. ADR 0009 is
preserved because ambiguity is visible at the Fillet consumer; other shared consumers are not
silently filtered. ADR 0011's explicit frame pairing is strengthened for signed principal Fillet
representatives, while the separate Plate defect remains honestly tracked. MFInstSeg was not
available and was not inspected; this child is not a transfer milestone.
